# Phase 6A: rates, quotations, deliveries, cement age

**Operations** in the top menu, for counter and stock staff.

## Today's rates
- One screen lists the cement and steel prices. Admin edits them and presses **Save**.
- The cement and steel already in your products were added automatically. Add or remove products at the bottom of the screen.
- Every change is kept, with who made it and the old price (shown as "was ₹…").
- **Share rates on WhatsApp**, in Telugu or English, makes a message like:

  > ₹425/bag … including GST

  WhatsApp then asks whom to send it to. Pick your contractors or a WhatsApp group (WhatsApp doesn't allow sending to a list automatically).

## Quotations and estimates
- **+ New quotation**: customer, phone, address, site, validity (15 days by default), then items with quantity, rate (blank = today's price) and discount.
- **+ Full House estimate**: the same, grouped by stage:
  1. Foundation & structure
  2. Plumbing & wiring
  3. Tiles & walls
  4. Final coat

  The print shows a subtotal for each stage.
- Each quote gets a number (QTN/26-27/000001) and can be printed, shared on WhatsApp, edited while open, or cancelled.
- **Make bill** turns the quote into a bill in one go. Stock, discount limits and the credit limit are checked as for any bill.
  - If stock is short, the quote stays open and you're told what is short.
  - If prices changed since the quote, staff bill at today's prices. Admin can keep the quoted prices.

## Deliveries
- Sales → open a bill → **Delivery…**: choose how much of each item goes in this trip, plus vehicle, driver, delivery charge and site address. It gets a challan number (DC/26-27/000001), and the challan prints on A4.
- **Partial deliveries**: one bill can go in several trips. The screen shows what is still left to deliver, and returns are taken off.
- Operations → **Deliveries** shows the day's trips (or *All not yet delivered*). Each moves from **Pending** to **Out for delivery** to **Delivered** (with who received it), or **Cancel**, which frees the quantity again.
- **GST note:** to charge GST on a delivery charge, add it as a line on the bill. The amount on the challan is for information only.

## Cement age (oldest first)
- On a purchase, cement lines have two new boxes: **Batch** and **packed** date (as printed on the bags).
- Operations → **Cement age** shows what is on hand, oldest first. Over 60 days is flagged (Settings: oldStockDays).
- Bags are assumed to go out oldest first.
- On the billing screen, a cement line shows "Send oldest first: batch … packed … (N days)".
- Cement products are tracked automatically; add others with "Track stock age for another product".
